# OSDI submission baseline

Checked on 2026-09-18 against the official
[OSDI '26 Call for Papers](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/call-for-papers)
and [requirements for authors](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/call-for-papers/requirements-authors).
OSDI '26 is a historical baseline, not a claim that this draft complies with
an unpublished future call.

## Frozen OSDI '26 baseline

- US letter, two columns, 10-point type on 12-point leading.
- Text block 7 by 9 inches with a 0.33-inch column gap.
- Submission body limit: 12 pages; references are unlimited.
- Double-blind review: anonymous author and affiliation fields.
- Keep page numbers in the review PDF.
- A paper of six pages or fewer would generally be difficult to evaluate as a
  full OSDI paper; this is guidance, not permission to pad the manuscript.

`main.tex` uses the official `usenix2019_v3.sty`; it does not add custom
geometry or substitute font packages. This internal-review draft intentionally
marks unmeasured performance and live-runtime claims as planned work.

## Recheck for the target CFP

Before submission, record the target year and recheck:

- [ ] official CFP and author-requirements URLs, deadlines, and eligibility;
- [ ] page/body/reference limits and whether appendices are allowed;
- [ ] paper size, font, leading, text block, column gap, and required template;
- [ ] anonymity, conflicts, author-list freeze, registration, and ethics rules;
- [ ] page-number policy and PDF/font validation requirements;
- [ ] artifact, disclosure, prior-publication, and concurrent-submission rules;
- [ ] title/abstract metadata and any mandatory topic or submission fields;
- [ ] final PDF body-page count and clean-build log.
